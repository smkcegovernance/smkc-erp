### Rationale

*   **Primary (`Sangli Red`):** A strong, deep red drawn directly from the logo's core. It's professional and commands attention without being overly aggressive.
*   **Secondary (`Miraj Gold`):** Inspired by the elegant gold ring, this color adds a touch of sophistication and warmth. It serves as an excellent complement to the primary red.
*   **Accent/CTA (`Kupwad Coral`):** A slightly brighter, more energetic shade of red for call-to-action buttons and important highlights. It stands out against the other colors while remaining harmonious with the overall theme.
*   **Neutrals:** A clean white base, a subtle gray for backgrounds, a readable gray for body text, and a dark, rich blue for headings provide a balanced and modern foundation. This ensures content is legible and the overall design feels clean and structured.
